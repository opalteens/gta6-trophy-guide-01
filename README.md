Leonida Nights is an offline, in-browser trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5: all 55 official trophies with their official names, descriptions and grades, a guide in our own words for each one that cites its sources, a checklist saved in the browser with export and import, search and filters by kind, grade and earned state, a spoiler-safe setting that hides story trophies until they are earned, a three-stage roadmap to the platinum of about 200 hours, and the one missable trophy — Actions Speak Louder — flagged clearly. Everything runs from static files under `app/` with no network requests; your progress never leaves your machine. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Pick a trophy to read its guide, tick off the ones you have earned, and follow the roadmap to the platinum.
